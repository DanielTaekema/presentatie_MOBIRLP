Does the package hold? Coherent, consistent, congruent and feasible

Coherence

The three subgoals reinforce each other. Tensions lie in the means, not in the goals.

Consistency

Zoning, homes, routes and hub all lower car dependence. The group and the agreement align the actors.

Congruence

All six serve the 10-minute city. Weakest where the housing allowance and fleet size may fall short.
